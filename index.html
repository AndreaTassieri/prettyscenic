About Us

We Build the World Behind Every Production

Pretty Scenic is a professional scenic construction company specialising in building, installing, and striking sets for theatre, television, and film productions. With extensive hands-on experience across studio and location builds throughout the UK, we deliver the precision craftsmanship that productions demand — on time and on budget.

From assembling stage productions to dismounting broadcast sets, our team works closely with production designers and art departments to bring creative visions to life — and take them down cleanly when the cameras stop rolling.
